- Afternoon: airport transfer by minibus, check in and rest.

- Stay: Hotel Rinia Central, Tirana (low estimate) or Hotel Illyria Beach, Durrës (high estimate). Both are step-free.

### Day 2: Tirana highlights and Bunk'Art

### Day 3: Wine and olive oil farm lunch
