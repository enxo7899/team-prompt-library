## Overview
Two parts of this request cannot be built. Theth hikes run from June to September only, so there is no Theth hike in February, and Dubrovnik is in Croatia, which is outside the countries we plan. Below is a four-day Durrës-based plan with the closest options we do sell: Berat on foot and a day trip to Kotor in Montenegro.

## Day by day
### Day 1: Arrival in Durrës
- Morning and afternoon: your flight to Tirana.
- Evening: airport transfer (TR-01) and check in.
- Stay: Villa Adriatik or Hotel Illyria Beach, Durrës.

### Day 2: Berat
- Morning: depart for Berat (DT-06, 10 hours in total, drive included).
- Afternoon: old quarter on foot and wine tasting.
- Evening: back in Durrës.
- Stay: Villa Adriatik or Hotel Illyria Beach, Durrës.

### Day 3: Kotor, Montenegro
- Morning: depart for Kotor (DT-10, 14 hours in total, drive included). Passports are required.
- Afternoon: Kotor old town.
- Evening: back in Durrës.
- Stay: Villa Adriatik or Hotel Illyria Beach, Durrës.

### Day 4: Durrës old town and departure
- Morning: Durrës old town and amphitheatre walk (DT-01, 3 hours).
- Afternoon: airport transfer (TR-01), timed to your flight.
- Evening: home.
