CONTRACT TEXT:
ALLOTMENT AGREEMENT between Illyria Beach Hotel sh.p.k. ("Hotel") and Teuta Travel sh.p.k. ("Agency").
1. Allotment. The Hotel reserves 10 double rooms per night for the Agency from 1 April 2027 to 31 October 2027 at EUR 82 per room per night, breakfast included.
2. Deposit. The Agency pays a non-refundable deposit of EUR 6,000, equal to 40% of the estimated season value, within 7 days of signing. Payment to account AL47 2121 1009 0000 0002 3569 8741.
3. Stay invoices. Each stay is invoiced at check-in and is payable on the day of check-out.
4. Release. The Agency may release unsold rooms free of charge up to 21 days before arrival. Rooms released later are charged at the first night.
5. Prices. Room prices are fixed for the season. The municipal tourist tax is added to each invoice at the rate set by the municipality.
6. Hotel unable to provide rooms. If the Hotel cannot provide a confirmed room, it will offer a room in a comparable hotel nearby. The Hotel has no further obligation.
7. Liability. The Hotel's total liability under this agreement is limited to the amount paid for the stay concerned.
8. Term. The agreement runs from signing until 31 October 2027 and renews automatically for a further season unless either party gives written notice at least 90 days before expiry.
9. Law. This agreement is governed by Albanian law. Disputes go to the courts of Durrës.
