TRANSPORT SERVICES AGREEMENT between Adriatic Coaches d.o.o. ("Operator") and Teuta Travel sh.p.k. ("Agency").
1. Services. The Operator provides up to two 50-seat coaches with driver for Agency tours from 1 January to 31 December 2027, at EUR 480 per coach per day.
2. Payment. The Agency pays the full estimated monthly total on the 1st of the month before the month of service. Unused days are not refunded.
3. Cancellation by the Agency. Cancellation less than 7 days before a service day is charged at 100%. Cancellation 7 to 14 days before is charged at 50%.
4. Cancellation by the Operator. If the Operator cannot provide a coach, it will provide a replacement coach of equal size at its own cost and refund the day's fee for any day not served.
5. Fuel surcharge. The Operator may add a fuel surcharge to any invoice by written notice. The surcharge is not capped.
6. Insurance. The Operator holds passenger liability insurance of EUR 1,000,000 per incident and will provide the certificate on request.
7. Term. This agreement ends on 31 December 2027 and does not renew.
8. Law. This agreement is governed by Italian law. Disputes are settled by arbitration in Bari.
